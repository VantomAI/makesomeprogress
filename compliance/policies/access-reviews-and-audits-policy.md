# Access Reviews and Audits Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines how Make Some Progress reviews and audits access to systems that store, process, or administer consumer data.

## Scope

This policy applies to GitHub, Supabase, Plaid, domain/hosting accounts, and any related administrative systems.

## Policy

The application owner performs periodic access reviews to confirm that access is still appropriate and limited to authorized users.

Reviews include:

- Supabase project members and service credentials.
- GitHub repository collaborators and deployment permissions.
- Plaid dashboard users and API keys.
- Edge Function secrets and environment configuration.
- Authentication settings and row-level security policies.

Any unexpected, unused, or unnecessary access must be removed. Any suspicious access activity must be investigated promptly.

## Frequency

Access reviews are performed at least quarterly and after any security incident, contributor change, or material change to sensitive data handling.

## Records

The owner maintains a simple record of review date, systems reviewed, issues found, and remediation completed.
