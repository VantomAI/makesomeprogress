# Access Deprovisioning and Modification Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines how access is changed or removed when a user, contributor, administrator, or integration no longer requires access.

## Scope

This policy applies to Supabase, GitHub, Plaid, hosting systems, and any account used to administer Make Some Progress.

## Policy

Access must be modified or removed promptly when it is no longer required.

For a single-owner application, this includes:

- Removing former collaborators from GitHub.
- Removing former project members from Supabase.
- Removing unauthorized Plaid dashboard access.
- Rotating keys or secrets when compromise or unnecessary exposure is suspected.
- Disabling or unlinking Plaid connections when requested by the user.

## Timeline

High-risk or administrative access should be removed as soon as practical, ideally within 24 hours of identification. Routine access changes should be completed within 5 business days.

## Verification

After removal, the owner verifies that access is no longer active and records any key rotation or account removal performed.
