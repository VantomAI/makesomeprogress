# Internal Systems MFA Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines MFA requirements for internal systems that store, process, deploy, or administer consumer data.

## Scope

This policy applies to Supabase, GitHub, Plaid, hosting accounts, email accounts used for administration, and any other internal system used to operate Make Some Progress.

## Policy

Administrative access to internal systems must use MFA where supported.

Systems requiring MFA include:

- Supabase dashboard.
- GitHub account with repository access.
- Plaid dashboard.
- Email account used for administrative recovery or security notices.
- Any hosting, domain, or deployment provider account.

## Exceptions

If a system does not support MFA, the owner must use a strong unique password and reassess the system during access reviews.

## Review

MFA status is reviewed quarterly as part of access reviews.
